# Prompts and revisions

The initial draft's exact assistant transcript is unavailable. These are the task prompts and review questions used in this session; they are presented as a working record, not a verbatim transcript of any earlier session.

1. User brief: “Build a working AI-assisted tool, state a numbered business goal, show how it works, write Priya a memo, record a short walkthrough, and fill the form.”
2. Review question: “Does the existing app's repeat rate respect the policy's migrated UTC resolution timestamps and 30-day observation window?”
3. Review question: “Can a follow-up ticket still be open, and should the agent leaderboard count ticket creation week or resolution week?”
4. Review question: “Does the text holdout test prove topic quality, and was the vectorizer fitted only on training messages?”
5. Drafting request: “Explain the corrected baseline, potential quarterly value, assumptions, and omissions in plain language for Priya.”

Changes: the draft's 11.97%/Rs 72.9k case became 13.06%/Rs 74.9k after correcting time zones, open follow-ups, and incomplete 30-day windows. Text vectorization now fits only the training set. I discarded the idea of treating classifier agreement with intake tags as NMF topic accuracy.
